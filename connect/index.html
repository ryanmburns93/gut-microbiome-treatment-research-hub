Our mission

Gut Brain Biome started with one person's gut-brain condition and a search for better answers. It brings together three things:

  • Our story. A personal record of the condition, the treatments tried, and the journey so far, in Our Story.
  • The research. Peer-reviewed studies on therapies that target the gut microbiome, summarized clearly and linked to primary sources, in Research, with background articles in Resources.
  • The community. Support networks, engaging resources, and ongoing efforts toward discovery, right here.

How the research is curated

  • Sources first. Every study links to the original paper, with its authors, journal, and year.
  • Honest summaries. Each one has a plain-language conclusion and a note on why it matters and where it falls short.
  • Clear study types. Reviews, trials, and animal studies are labeled so early signals aren't mistaken for settled findings.
  • Kept current. New studies are added from our curation spreadsheet, and the site updates within the hour.

Looking for research databases and trial registries? See Resources.

Affiliate disclosure

This site contains a few links to books or tests we have valued, which may use affiliate programs. This is an attempt to recoup the cost of hosting this important information and sharing the experiences that have led to radical quality-of-life improvements for us. We believe it is vital to share our journey regardless of financial return, and we are fortunate to have been in a financial position to pursue experimental treatment.

Any concerns about financial gain pale in the face of the cost of medical care for chronic illness in the United States. If affiliate links are a concern, we encourage anyone to use this information regardless, and we hope others find value in hearing about our journey out of the dark, forgotten corners of society where chronic sickness often leads.

Most importantly, we hope others will support the brave and intelligent patients and researchers who came before us. They helped us find an effective way forward for Monica's mental health treatment, one that seems to target the root causes of bipolar disorder, after years of being let down by traditional pharmaceuticals and mental health practitioners who chased symptoms and resigned Monica to a life of incessant suffering and pain.
