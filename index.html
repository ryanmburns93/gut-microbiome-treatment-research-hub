Our research standards

Curated by hand

Every study is read and summarized individually, and always linked to the original paper.

Honest about limits

Each summary says why the study matters and where it falls short, such as small samples, animal models, or industry ties.

Clear about study type

Reviews, trials, and animal studies are labeled, so early signals aren't mistaken for settled findings.

Written for everyone

Plain-language conclusions up front, with authors, journal, and a link to the source for researchers and clinicians.
