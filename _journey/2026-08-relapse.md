By August 9 Monica's depressed mood was severe again and she was sleeping 11 to 20 hours a day.

Our best guess is that her gut slipped back toward dysbiosis. We didn't keep to the high-fiber diet that helps transplanted microbes take hold, and atypical antipsychotics may themselves disrupt the gut microbiome. She took the stool we had left from April, and we ordered fresh.
