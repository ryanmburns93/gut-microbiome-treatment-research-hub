By December 2025 we had tried nearly everything. A year or two earlier I had read about [Jane Dudley](https://www.microbiomeinmind.com.au/), an Australian woman who treated her bipolar I disorder with a fecal microbiota transplant in 2016. Now, with little left to try, something as dramatic as FMT was on the table.
